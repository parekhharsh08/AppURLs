1. Introduction

Welcome to Tic Tac Toe ("the App"), developed and published by InfinityTech ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

2. Information Collection & Usage

A. Personal Information

We do not collect or request any personal identification information (such as your name, email, phone number, address, or contacts). The app can be played 100% offline without creating an account.

B. Device & Gameplay Data (Stored Locally)

Settings (sound and haptic effects) and match statistics (win/loss records and streaks) are saved locally on your device via standard Android offline storage. This data is never uploaded to any cloud server.

C. Advertising & Diagnostic Data (Third Parties)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Android ID)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Coarse location (country/city derived from IP address)
  • Ad performance and diagnostic metrics

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can manage your consent preferences at any time within the App Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you have any concerns, please contact our support team immediately.
