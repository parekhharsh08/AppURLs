## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information directly.**
- We do not require account creation, registration, or login.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts.
- We do not access your microphone, camera, photos, or storage files.

### B. Local Device Data (Offline Storage)
The App stores game-related preferences and offline statistics locally on your device using Android DataStore / Room:
- Sound and haptic vibration preferences
- Match statistics (games played, win rates, win streaks, difficulty levels defeated)

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time via the **Reset All Statistics** button in the Settings screen or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.

### C. Automatically Collected Information via Third-Party Services
The App is supported by advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. To deliver and personalize advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device data:
- **Device Identifiers:** Advertising ID (e.g., Google AAID), Android ID, IP address.
- **Device & Software Information:** Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale/language.
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Views, clicks, impressions, and diagnostic data related to ad performance.
